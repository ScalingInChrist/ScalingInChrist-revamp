Campaign Types

The Google Ads Formats We Use and Why

Not every campaign type is right for every business. We pick the ones that match your goals and budget.

Search Ads

Search Campaigns

Text ads that appear at the top of Google when someone searches for your keywords. The most direct way to capture buyer intent.

  • Shows up when customers are actively searching
  • Pay only when someone clicks your ad
  • Best for: service businesses, appointments, calls
  • Results: often within the first week
Local Services Ads

Google Local Services

The "Google Guaranteed" badge ads that show above regular search results. You pay per lead, not per click — and Google vets businesses before showing them.

  • Only available to verified, licensed businesses
  • Pay per lead, not per click
  • Google Guaranteed badge builds instant trust
  • Best for: plumbers, roofers, HVAC, cleaners
Display & Retargeting

Display & Remarketing

Visual banner ads that follow people who visited your website across millions of sites. Keeps your business top of mind until they're ready to book.

  • Re-engage visitors who didn't convert
  • Show across Google's massive display network
  • Very low cost per impression
  • Best for: higher-ticket services with longer decisions

Key Metrics We Track

What "Working" Actually Looks Like

These are the numbers we watch every single week on your behalf.

CPC Cost Per Click How much you're paying every time someone clicks your ad. We work to lower this over time.
CTR Click-Through Rate What % of people who see your ad actually click it. Higher CTR = better ad relevance.
QS Quality Score Google's 1–10 rating of your ads. Higher QS = lower costs and better ad positions.
CVR Conversion Rate What % of visitors take action (call, fill out a form). We optimize your landing page for this.
CPL Cost Per Lead The most important number. Total ad spend divided by total leads. This is what we drive down.
ROAS Return on Ad Spend For every $1 you put in, how much you're getting back. Our goal: make this number grow every month.

Common Questions

Things We Get Asked All the Time

How much should I budget for Google Ads?

For most local service businesses, we recommend starting with $500–$1,500/month in ad spend. The right number depends on your market, competition, and average job value. We'll tell you exactly what makes sense for your niche before you commit to anything.

How fast will I see results?

Many clients get their first calls within the first week. However, full optimization takes 30–60 days as we gather real data on what's converting in your specific market. We set expectations honestly — no promises of overnight miracles.

Do I need a good website for Google Ads to work?

Yes. Google Ads brings traffic — but your website converts that traffic into leads. If your site is slow, hard to navigate, or doesn't have a clear call to action, you're paying for clicks that bounce. We can handle both if needed.

What's the difference between Google Ads and SEO?

SEO builds organic (free) rankings over 6–12 months. Google Ads gives you instant paid placement. Both are valuable — ads for fast results now, SEO for long-term foundation. Most growing businesses need both eventually.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. We work on a monthly basis. We'd rather earn your business every month by delivering results than lock you in with a contract. That said, Google Ads does take 60–90 days to fully optimize, so we encourage clients to commit to at least 3 months to see the full picture.
