The Full Funnel Approach

How a Real Meta Ads Campaign is Structured

Most people run one ad to everyone and wonder why it doesn't work. We build campaigns that guide people through a proper journey.

Top of Funnel — Awareness

Cold Audience Ads

Reach new people who've never heard of you. Short video or strong visual that introduces your business and your value — no hard sell yet. Goal: build awareness and collect data.

Middle of Funnel — Consideration

Warm Engagement Retargeting

Show follow-up ads to people who watched your video, visited your website, or engaged with your page. Now we introduce social proof — reviews, before/afters, specifics of what you do.

Bottom of Funnel — Conversion

Hot Retargeting — The Ask

Target people who visited your services page or got close to contacting you. Now we make a direct offer — free estimate, special promotion, a clear reason to reach out today.

Scaling Layer

Lookalike & Advantage+ Scaling

Once we know who converts, we build Lookalike audiences from your customer list and use Meta's AI to find more people just like them — at scale.

Pixel & Tracking

You Can't Optimize What You Can't Measure

Most Meta ads underperform because the tracking is broken. We fix this first — always.

🔬

Meta Pixel Installation

We install the pixel correctly, verify all key events are firing (PageView, Lead, Contact, Purchase), and test it across your entire website before any ads go live.

🖥️

Conversions API (CAPI)

Browser-based tracking is increasingly blocked by iOS updates and ad blockers. CAPI sends conversion data directly from your server to Meta — much more reliable and accurate.

📞

Call & Form Tracking

Phone calls and form fills are your actual leads — not just clicks. We set up proper tracking so every lead is attributed to the exact campaign that generated it.

📋

Event Match Quality (EMQ)

Meta scores how well your pixel data matches actual customer records. A higher EMQ means better audience matching and lower cost per result. We monitor and improve this score.

Common Questions

Things We Get Asked All the Time

How much budget do I need to start?

We recommend starting with at least $500–$1,000/month in ad spend. Below that, the algorithm doesn't have enough data to optimize properly and results can be inconsistent. That said, we've gotten strong results for clients starting with as little as $20/day in specific local markets.

Do I need to create my own videos?

It helps — authentic, phone-shot content from your actual job sites consistently outperforms polished stock imagery. We'll give you a simple content brief that takes 10 minutes to shoot. If you truly can't create content, we can work with what you have or help arrange creative production.

How long before I see results?

Some clients get leads within the first week. But we set honest expectations: the first 30 days is the learning phase. Real, consistent results typically kick in around week 4–6 once the pixel has data and we've identified the best-performing ads.

What's the difference between Facebook and Instagram ads?

They're managed through the same platform (Meta Ads Manager) and share the same targeting. Facebook tends to have slightly older audiences and lower CPMs. Instagram has higher engagement, especially for visual businesses. We typically run on both and let the data tell us where to put more budget.

My previous agency ran Meta Ads and it didn't work. Why would this be different?

The most common reasons Meta Ads fail: broken tracking (so the algorithm can't optimize), weak creative (so no one stops scrolling), bad offer (nothing compelling to act on), or no funnel structure (one cold ad trying to do too much). We audit all four before spending a dollar and fix whatever is broken.
